

# Generate Custom App Logo for Jobber Marketplace

## Overview
Create a professional 384x384 app logo for your Jobber marketplace listing that matches the Halo Cadence Flow branding and conveys automation/bot capabilities for service businesses.

## Logo Design Concept

### Visual Elements
- **Icon**: Abstract representation combining automation (circular flow/gears) with the "Halo" concept (ring/circular motif)
- **Style**: Modern, clean, minimalist - matching your Apple-inspired UI
- **Shape**: Rounded square (matching your `rounded-xl` design pattern) or circular icon

### Color Palette
| Element | Color | Hex |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Primary Background | Deep Navy | `#1e293b` |
| Accent/Icon | Professional Blue | `#3b82f6` |
| Secondary Accent | Teal Green | `#10b981` |
| Highlights | White | `#ffffff` |

### Design Options
1. **Abstract Flow Mark**: Circular arrows or waves representing "Cadence" and automation flow
2. **Bot + Halo Hybrid**: Stylized robot head with a glowing ring/halo
3. **Geometric Mark**: Interlocking shapes suggesting integration and workflow

## Implementation

### Step 1: Generate Logo with AI
Use the Lovable AI image generation to create a professional logo with this prompt:
> "Professional app logo, 384x384 pixels, deep navy background (#1e293b), featuring a modern abstract circular flow symbol in bright blue (#3b82f6) with teal accent (#10b981), representing automation and workflow for service businesses, clean minimalist design, suitable for app marketplace, no text"

### Step 2: Save and Use
- The generated logo will be saved to the project
- Can be used directly in your Jobber app submission
- Also usable as an updated favicon for your platform

## Technical Details
- **Output Size**: 384x384 pixels (Jobber requirement)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency or solid background
- **Model**: `google/gemini-2.5-flash-image` for generation
